## 1. What FinOps IQ answers

FinOps IQ rates the client's FinOps capabilities on the FinOps Foundation's Crawl, Walk, Run scale (plus "Not yet"), sets each against a default target for the estate's complexity tier, and sizes the value of closing the gaps as a conservative, expected and upper range, net of implementation effort. It has two modes:

- **Executive** (20 questions): do we need to act now, and roughly how much is at stake? For the CIO or CFO. It replaces the former FinOps Value Case.
- **Workshop** (up to 69 questions, asked only where the estate makes them apply): what exactly, by when, owned by whom, and what is it worth? Consultant-led with the cloud, FinOps, finance and engineering leads. An executive assessment can be continued in the workshop with its answers carried over.

Scoring runs on the LTM service, never in the browser. A completed assessment is recorded and cannot be changed; start a new one to revise it.

## 3. How to talk about value

- Every figure carries the label: "Indicative value range: not a savings commitment; evidence validation required". Conservative, expected and upper cases, net of estimated internal implementation effort and tooling. External support is not included.
- Cases: expected = 50% of each lever's maximum rate, conservative = 25%; the upper case uses the maximum. Public cloud levers together are capped at 29% of in-scope public cloud spend (Flexera 2026 State of the Cloud Report: respondents' average self-estimate of wasted IaaS/PaaS spend (used as a ceiling only) [CLM-FIN-040, CLM-FIN-138]).
- No value figure is shown without your internal cost rate per FTE-month: net value cannot be computed without it. The levers below show what would be quantified.
- Say "indicative" and "range", and point to the evidence that would firm it up (section 7). Never present the range as a promise, as a sum already lost, or as a FinOps Foundation result. `scripts/check-claims.cjs` holds the phrases that must not appear in client-facing text.
- A blank answer or "Don't know" is rated as unconfirmed and never raises a value. The client's own estimate of waste is never used in a figure.

### Understand Usage & Cost

## 6. How the result is built

- **Severity** of a capability gap: 1 level below target = Medium; 2 levels below target = High; 3 levels below target = Critical. Raised one step for Data Ingestion, Allocation, FinOps Practice Operations from Tier 2.
- **Roadmap pace**: a gap moves one level at a time: the first level 3 months after it starts, each further level 6 months later.
- **Need verdict** (executive mode): a need index out of 100, raised by tier 2 (+10), tier 3 (+20), expected value at least 5% of in-scope public cloud spend (+10), growth at least 20% (+10), ai in scope (+10). Bands: up to 25 "Lightweight practices sufficient at current scale"; up to 50 "Targeted improvements needed now"; up to 75 "Material exposure: act this year"; up to 100 "Act now".
- **Appoint an accountable FinOps owner with a mandate** (High): raised when MP-01 is answered Not yet, Crawl, Don't know, not answered. Action: Appoint an accountable FinOps owner with a written mandate and an executive sponsor.
- **Sequence commitments after scheduling and Spot moves**: Steady usage exceeds today's commitment coverage, but the scheduling and Spot moves in the plan remove all of it; commitments bought now would lock in usage the plan removes. Action: Agree the sequence: scheduling and Spot moves first, then re-baseline commitment coverage on the usage that remains.

- **FinOps assessment and value validation** (Advisory, FinOps): Confirm the capability profile and turn indicative value into evidenced ranges with your own billing data. Indicative scope: 3–6 weeks.
- **FinOps operating model and governance** (Advisory, FinOps): Make someone accountable for technology spend, with a mandate, a cadence and policies that hold. Indicative scope: 4–8 weeks.
- **FinOps data, allocation and tooling implementation** (Implementation, FinOps with Cloud Transformation): Give every cost an accountable owner and complete, timely data on the platforms you use. Indicative scope: 6–12 weeks.

- **Unit economics, forecasting and chargeback** (Implementation, FinOps with Data and AI): Tie spend to business output, forecast it from its drivers and charge it to the units that use it. Indicative scope: 8–12 weeks.
