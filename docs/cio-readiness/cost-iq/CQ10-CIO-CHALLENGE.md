## Findings, ranked

### Critical

None found. Reason: nothing in the sampled client or consultant text is false, promises savings, exposes another client's data or contradicts the approved disclaimer. The items below are credibility and consistency defects.

### High

**H1. The executive summary contradicts itself on whether any saving is stated.**
Evidence: client p. 3 "Value range: total across the estate ... Expected GBP 2.49m ... Upper GBP 7.35m ... GBP 17.2m", then the Decision callout: "Sizing starts when the evidence arrives; until then no saving is stated." (`report.ts:213-215`). Same on the cloud-heavy sample (cloud line GBP 2.00m, same sentence).
Consequence: a CFO reads two incompatible statements on one page.
Fix: in `summary()`, when `nc` or a quantified cloud line exists, replace "until then no saving is stated" with: "Sizing of the findings starts when the evidence arrives; until then the only amounts in this report are the cloud line from FinOps IQ and the licence lever sized from the client's measured figures." Keep the current text only when no amount is shown. Add a test that the summary never contains "no saving is stated" when an amount table is present.

**H2. The cloud line cannot be tested: "capped at 29% of that spend" names no spend, and the headline total carries no confidence.**
Evidence: client p. 4 and p. 8 (text above); `report.ts:174-175`; `cost-iq-summary.ts:58` sets `cloudSpend` to `{low: null, high: null}` when `v.spend` is absent, which is the case for 7 of 9 reference scenarios I ran. The cloud line says "confidence Low" only in its small print; the total on p. 3 (GBP 17.2m upper) is labelled only "indicative".
Consequence: the CFO asks "29% of what?" and cannot tell whether 2.00m a year is plausible. The Low confidence disappears in the total.
Fix: (a) when spend is null, print "FinOps IQ recorded no in-scope spend figure for this line, so the cap cannot be shown as an amount" and do not write "of that spend"; (b) show the in-scope spend band from FinOps IQ whenever it holds one; (c) add to the total caption: "includes a cloud line of Low confidence" (read from `finops.confidence`); (d) add a year-by-year row set (Year 1, 2, 3) for the cloud line and the lever. UNVERIFIED by me: why FinOps IQ leaves `spend` null for a quantified result; the FinOps IQ owner should say whether this is by design.

**H3. The delivery plan asks the client for evidence for findings that were not raised.**
Evidence: on-premises sample (3 findings: spend baseline, application portfolio, cost allocation), client p. 7 lists "Evidence for 'Complexity and technical debt are contributing to run cost'" and "Evidence for 'Technology duplication after M&A may dilute synergy'". The mixed sample lists evidence for "Business perception of technology value is weak or mixed", "Transformation spend lacks measurable outcomes", "Technology workforce capacity may not align to demand", "Technology investment is difficult to redirect", none of which appears among its 10 findings. Cause: the library stores one fixed `prerequisites` list per package, with finding IDs in each line (e.g. `"Evidence for F-001 (...)"`) and the builder prints all of them.
Consequence: the client is asked for evidence behind findings the report never showed; M&A evidence for a client with no M&A question raised.
Fix: in the delivery-plan builder, keep a prerequisite line only if its finding ID is among the findings raised (the IDs are in the line text); keep the generic lines. Add a test: every quoted finding title under "Evidence for" appears under Findings in the same edition.

**H4. The report recommends scoping a FinOps IQ assessment that is already linked.**
Evidence: mixed and cloud-heavy samples, Finding 8 -> "Cloud Cost Management Scoping" (client pp. 9, 14-15, 19, 23): "A short scoping step that decides whether a FinOps IQ assessment of cloud cost management is warranted ... LTM runs the FinOps IQ assessment as the next step"; workstream "Plan the FinOps IQ assessment"; "Hand-off: Link the Cost IQ finding to the FinOps IQ assessment". Page 4 of the same report says the cloud line is "From the FinOps IQ assessment dated 2026-10-14, linked to this report on 2026-11-03".
Consequence: four places in one report tell the CIO to do what the report says is done; it also reads as upselling.
Fix: when a quantified FinOps IQ summary is linked, build R-003 as "Review the linked FinOps IQ result against Finding 8" (a one-week advisory step) or mark the package "Already covered by the linked FinOps IQ assessment of <date>". Keep the scoping text when nothing is linked.

### Medium

**M1. "Net of the cost to deliver" is the client's own internal effort only, and the report does not say what it leaves out.** Evidence: client p. 9 "One-off cost ... from the client's own cost of an FTE-month: GBP 30k"; A-09. Not in the net: LTM or third-party fees, tooling, early-termination or true-up penalties, internal time for the other nine packages. Fix: add one sentence under the lever table and in A-09: "Cost to deliver is the client's internal effort at its own rate. It excludes any external fees, tools and contract penalties, which are agreed separately."

**M2. The money tables do not show the phasing, nor which year-one rule was used.** Evidence: p. 3, p. 8, p. 9 show only net run rate and net three-year. A-09: "or a quarter when the client gave none" does not say which applied. Fix: add Year 1, Year 2, Year 3 columns (the page already shows Year 1 for the cloud line, `cost-iq-v10.html:353-354`) and a line "Year one used the client's renewal share of 60%" or "a quarter, no renewal data given".

**M3. Upper case equals 100% of the client's unused licence cost and the lever has no confidence label beside it.** Evidence: p. 9, "Upper GBP 1.00m ... up to 100%"; A-08 Low only in Appendix B. The range is bounded by the base (no inflation beyond the base), but a board will read the upper case as achievable. Fix: put "Confidence: Low (LTM heuristic, no published range)" under the lever table, and in the total caption; consider capping the upper case at a stated share (for example the share of contracts renewing within the three years) once renewal data is given.

**M4. "Where to look first" and headline scores can rest on one self-reported question.** Evidence: mixed sample p. 3 lists "Operations Economics 2 / 100, 1 question" (from one answer, 98% manual work, self-estimated); cloud-heavy lists Cloud Economics 20, Operations 21 and Vendor 25 on 1 question each; mixed p. 5 shows Cloud Economics 100/100 on one question beside FinOps IQ "Crawl" and "Walk" ratings on p. 8. A-02 weights every dimension equally regardless of questions. The report does caveat single-question dimensions (p. 5) but not in the exec summary. Fix: in the "Where to look first" table, exclude dimensions with fewer than two scored answers or tag them "one question: weak signal" (this is the open S5 item in PLAN CQ4); add a sentence in the Cloud and FinOps section where the Cost IQ cloud score and the FinOps IQ ratings diverge.

**M5. The executive summary has no recommendation or priority order, and the roadmap has no overall sequence.** Evidence: p. 3 gives scores and a generic decision; p. 9 lists nine steps unordered; pp. 10-18 give each package's elapsed time but no combined timeline, effort or order. FRAMEWORK 13(f) asks for situation, issue, recommendation, value with basis. Fix: add to the summary a "Recommended first steps" line derived from the engine (the packages for the High findings, in dependency order, with the combined elapsed range from `elapsed()`); label the roadmap table with its ordering rule.

**M6. Two sources are outside the trusted-sources policy or misdated.** Evidence: Appendix D lists "ServiceNow, Understanding CMDB Health (ServiceNow Community article, not product documentation)" and "SAP ... NetWeaver 7.3 EhP1 help set", both with Year "2026". The Year column is the year the page was checked for playbook sources (`report.ts:490`) but the publication year for GAO (2004), so the column means two things. FRAMEWORK source policy excludes blogs; a community article is in that class. Fix: replace the ServiceNow entry with a documentation page, or remove the CMDB entry; for SAP either find the current-release page or keep the entry with "consultant to confirm against the client's release" but do not list it as a source for a client; rename the column "Year published or checked" and add a "Checked" column.

**M7. The result screen disagrees with the report.** Evidence in `Accelerators/cost-iq-v10.html`: line 407 shows tiles "Evidence completeness" (the confidence word) and "Completeness", the wording CQ5 W7 removed from the report because it reads as data quality; "Cost IQ" and "Value IQ" scores appear bare (no "/ 100"), while the report says "technology cost management ... out of 100"; the "Initial signals" list takes `findings.slice(0,4)` in engine order, whereas the report numbers findings by severity; `cloudSummaryHtml` (line 351) prints the shared `boundaryStatement`, which the report no longer prints. Fix: rename the tiles "Questions answered" and drop the confidence tile; label "/ 100"; sort by severity; replace the boundary statement with a Cost IQ sentence.

**M8. The consultant edition is the default and the primary button, with no role check.** Evidence: `handler.ts:44` (`view = q.view === 'client' ? 'client' : 'consultant'`); page button "Report (PDF)" is the consultant edition (line 421). The consultant edition contains the raw answers, the consultant notes and the draft statement of work. If a CIO self-serve user can reach this page, they download it first. Not verified: whether any non-LTM user can hold Cost IQ access today (platform P1). Fix: label the buttons "Consultant edition (PDF)" and "Client edition (PDF)", make the client edition the primary action, and (if clients can sign in) require a consultant role for `view=consultant`.

**M9. The page loads third-party resources the data notice does not mention.** Evidence: `cost-iq-v10.html:8` (Chart.js from `cdn.jsdelivr.net`, no integrity attribute), `:26-28` (Google Fonts). For EU and UK visitors this sends their IP to those hosts. The legal position is UNSOURCED here (counsel to say); the engineering fix does not depend on it. Fix: self-host the script and fonts under `/assets`, or add integrity and a line to the data notice.

**M10. Non-cloud estates get a zero-money report and are told to "run FinOps IQ".** Evidence: on-premises sample p. 3 and p. 6: "Cloud line: not quantified ... Run FinOps IQ and link it" twice, "No amount is stated". For a client with no public cloud described this is irrelevant advice and leaves the CFO with no value indication. Fix: when the footprint has no PUBLIC_CLOUD, SAAS or AI, print "Not applicable: no public cloud or SaaS estate was described" instead of the warning; and say once, on the summary, what evidence would let LEV-11 be sized ("enter the measured unused licence cost").

**M11. Finding counts and service names are inconsistent.** Evidence: Findings 3 and 4 are "raised by" the same SaaS-overlap answer; Findings 5 and 8 by the same commitment-flexibility answer (client pp. 6-7); "10 findings, 6 high" therefore counts four answers twice, and a CIO will see the same quotation. The backlog CSV's "LTM service" column ("Technology Operating Model", "Evidence-Led Cost & Value Assessment", "SaaS & Software Optimization") differs from the report's offering names ("Technology Cost and Value Governance", "Benefits Realisation Framework"), and the CSV is finding-level, not the platform-specific actions FRAMEWORK describes for the technical backlog. Fix: add "shares its trigger with Finding n" under such findings; use the offering name from the delivery library in the CSV; add the playbook practice names as a second CSV or column.
