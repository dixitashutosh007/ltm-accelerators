# Phase 3 automated scoring audit

Generated by `npx tsx packages/engine-core/audit/run.ts` (800 random questionnaires per engine; sensitivity from 30 base questionnaires plus any uniform baselines the engine declares, varying one question at a time over all its answers and "unanswered").

How to read it:
- **Range**: values outside the declared scale are bugs.
- **Bands**: a band that random answers almost never reach, or that the best or worst possible answers cannot reach, needs recalibration.
- **Sensitivity**: "largest single-answer swing" is the most one answer can move the headline. "Share" is that swing as a share of the headline's observed range; above about 0.5 means one answer dominates.
- **Dead questions** change nothing the client sees.
- **Missing data**: a positive shift means leaving answers blank makes the result look *better* (flattering).
