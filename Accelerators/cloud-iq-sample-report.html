1. Executive Context & Diagnosis

Meridian Financial Group has successfully adopted cloud infrastructure for customer-facing web services, but now faces a classic "hybrid transformation stall." Today, 62% of the compute estate sits across AWS and Azure, while core transaction ledgers remain tethered to two aging data centres in London Slough and Frankfurt.

At 52 / 100 (2.6 / 5.0), Meridian’s overall cloud maturity lags the tier-1 financial services peer median of 64 / 100. This is not a technical failure of engineering talent; it is an organizational and economic bottleneck where legacy data centre operating expenses ($18.5M/yr) and unmanaged cloud sprawl ($14.2M/yr) compete for the same capital.

2. The Financial Friction: $3.85M Annual Cost Leakage

Our forensic scan identified $3,850,000 in annual addressable cost leakage:

Key Takeaway for the CFO: $2.13M of this leakage can be captured within 90 days with zero operational risk by eliminating zombie compute resources and rightsizing commitment tiers (1–3 year Savings Plans). This captured capital directly self-funds the application migration factory needed to vacate the London Slough data centre.

3. Acute Time Horizon: The 10-Month DC Lease Cliff

The primary constraint driving immediate executive action is the impending expiration of the London Slough colocation lease in 10 months. Failure to decommission or vacate before this deadline triggers an automatic 24-month lease renewal penalty of $2.4M at escalated commercial rates. An industrialised "Migration Factory" approach must commence immediately in Month 2 to ensure total exit before Month 9.

4. Regulatory Exposure: DORA & SecNumCloud

Under the Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) and French sovereign cloud expectations (SecNumCloud 3.2), Meridian currently runs critical wealth telemetry through US-controlled cloud regions without tenant-isolated hardware encryption. A failure to partition European institutional accounts into a sovereign enclave risks regulatory scrutiny and potential commercial disqualification in French and German institutional mandates.

5. Three Irreversible Executive Bets

We recommend the Executive Committee authorize three immediate decisions:

  1. Execute FinOps Commitment Arbitrage (Days 1–30): Commit to 70% baseline Compute Savings Plans and freeze on-premise hardware refreshes, capturing $1.3M+ in immediate run-rate reduction.
  2. Mobilize the DC Migration Factory (Days 30–90): Partner with LTM to deploy an automated migration factory targeting the 76 workloads in Slough DC to beat the 10-month lease cliff.
  3. Establish a French Sovereign Enclave (Days 90–180): Partition regulated European investor telemetry into a SecNumCloud-qualified cloud enclave to achieve total DORA defensibility.
