⚠️ Key Architectural Contradictions Identified

⚡ Probe 1: The "Tagging Policy" vs Shared Infrastructure Gap
Meridian enforces a tagging policy on AWS EC2 and Azure VMs (reported 82% coverage), but over $480,000/month in NAT Gateway traffic, cross-AZ transit gateways, and unattached storage carries zero tags and is dumped into corporate overhead.
📦 Probe 2: The Multi-Tenant Kubernetes Blindspot
Meridian's digital banking platform operates on multi-tenant EKS and AKS clusters across Frankfurt and Dublin, but costs are tracked as a single lumped VM invoice. Pod resource requests exceed actual utilization by 3.8x with zero namespace chargeback.
⏳ Probe 3: The Zombie Commitment Expiration Risk
$1.2M/month in 3-year EC2 Reserved Instances will expire within 90 days. Meridian manages renewals in static spreadsheets without an automated purchasing ladder, exposing the bank to an immediate 34% compute rate shock ($408k/mo) upon expiration.
