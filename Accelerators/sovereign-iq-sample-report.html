Executive Summary · The Meridian Sovereignty Dilemma

Meridian Financial Group has successfully built a modern, resilient multi-cloud architecture across AWS London and Azure Dublin, delivering high availability for its wealth management and algorithmic portfolio platforms. However, our diagnostic reveals an acute, board-level contradiction between physical data residency and legal/jurisdictional sovereignty.

Critical Diagnostic Finding: Meridian’s primary cloud agreements are contracted under Delaware/California corporate parent paper, and all data-at-rest encryption relies on cloud provider-managed keys (AWS KMS / Azure PMK). Under the US CLOUD Act (18 U.S.C. § 2713) and FISA Section 702, US federal authorities can compel the hyperscalers to deliver cleartext access to Meridian's UK citizen wealth portfolios—bypassing the UK Investigatory Powers Act and directly violating EU GDPR Article 48 and DORA Article 28.

With EU DORA in active statutory enforcement, UK Operational Resilience transition ending March 31, 2025, and the EU AI Act high-risk enforcement date arriving August 2, 2026 for Meridian’s proprietary wealth recommendation LLM, physical colocation alone is no longer a defensible compliance strategy. A structured 4-phase transformation to Hold-Your-Own-Key (HYOK) and sovereign legal boundary separation is required.
